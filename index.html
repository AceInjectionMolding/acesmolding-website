Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

What services does Ace's Injection Molding offer?

We offer nine integrated manufacturing services under one roof: plastic injection molding, mold making, rapid prototyping, 3D printing (standard, large format, and high-temp resin), 3D printed injection molds, CO2 laser engraving, and fiber laser marking. Everything from prototype to production happens in our Bohemia, NY facility.

Do you handle small production runs?

Yes. We specialize in small to medium production runs that larger shops often turn away. With no minimum order requirements, we regularly produce batches of 100 to 10,000+ parts. Our in-house tooling and flexible scheduling make small runs cost-effective.

How do I get a quote from Ace's Injection Molding?

Send us your CAD files, drawings, or even a rough sketch through our contact form or email us at paul@acesmolding.com. We typically respond with a detailed quote including tooling options, per-part pricing, and lead times within 48 hours. You can also call us directly at (631) 891-9855.

Where is Ace's Injection Molding located?

We're located in Bohemia, Long Island, New York (ZIP 11716). We serve customers across Long Island, the greater New York metro area, New Jersey, Connecticut, and ship nationwide.
