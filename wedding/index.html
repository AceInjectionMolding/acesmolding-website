Taste of Portugal

What to Eat

  • 🥧 Pastel de Nata — get one at Manteigaria and one at Pastéis de Belém, then argue about which is better
  • 🐟 Bacalhau — Portugal has "100+ ways" to make it, we won't judge which you like best
  • 🦐 Fresh grilled fish & seafood — Ramiro is the legend, reservations advised
  • 🍖 Bifanas & piri-piri chicken — casual, cheap, incredible
  • 🍷 Ginjinha — a sour cherry liqueur, order it "com" (with a cherry in the cup), just for fun
  • 🍇 Vinho Verde & Douro reds — ask your waiter, you won't go wrong
Timing tip: July is peak sardine season — look for Santo António festival grills along Alfama's lanes.

Beyond the city

Day Trips

Best on Thursday or Friday daytime, before the welcome party.

Sintra

Pena Palace, Quinta da Regaleira, Moorish Castle — fairytale hills, about 40 min from the hotel.

Cascais & Guincho

Coastal town + wild Atlantic beach. Stop at Cabo da Roca, the westernmost point of Europe.

Setúbal & Arrábida

Pristine beaches, choco frito, and a ferry ride to the Tróia peninsula.
